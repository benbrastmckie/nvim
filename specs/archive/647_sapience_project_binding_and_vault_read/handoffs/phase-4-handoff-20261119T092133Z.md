## What NOT to Try
- Do not attempt to shadow `sapience_post`/`sapience_get` by sourcing the script-under-test into
  the test's own shell -- none of these scripts are designed to be sourced-and-overridden; they
  are standalone executables invoked via `bash <script>`.
- Do not invoke a script under test without first isolating `HOME` (to a directory with no real
  `~/.sapience/config.json`) AND either stubbing `curl` or setting `SAPIENCE_TOKEN` explicitly --
  an early draft of Case 1 omitted both and made one real, accidental GET call against the live
  vault. See the Phase 4 issue-log entry (costly, accidental-live-network-call).
