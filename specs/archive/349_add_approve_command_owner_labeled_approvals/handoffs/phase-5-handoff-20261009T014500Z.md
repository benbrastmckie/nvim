## Key Decisions Made

- **Recommendation vocabulary**: the classifier emits only `approve` (class `mechanical`) or
  `read-full-text` (class `declaration-level` or `needs-full-text`). `decline` remains a valid
  vocabulary value — it's one of `skill-approve`'s Stage B `AskUserQuestion` options in Phase 7
  — but the classifier itself never emits it automatically, since declining is the owner's
  judgment call, not something a mechanical check has a basis to infer. Documented explicitly
  in the script's own header comment and annotated on the plan's checklist (deviation: altered,
  not skipped — the vocabulary is honored, just not exercised in that one direction by this
  script).
- Boilerplate-line matcher uses a closed, enumerated prefix set (blank, `--`, `/-`, `-/`, `*`,
  `@[...]`) rather than a full Lean tokenizer — adequate for hand-built fixtures and the
  Phase 8 suite; a line that doesn't match any of those six forms is NOT boilerplate (so
  `all_boilerplate` goes to 0 and the item falls through to `declaration-level`), matching the
  "anything unrecognized classifies as declaration-level, never mechanical" rule.
- `decl_restated` = names appearing in BOTH the added and removed name sets for one item's diff;
  `decl_added`/`decl_removed` are the respective set differences.

## Deviations from Plan

## What NOT to Try

- Do not try to make the classifier emit `decline` by inventing a heuristic for "suspicious
  removal" — that is exactly the kind of impressionistic judgment the confidence vocabulary
  ruling forbids laundering as a measurement.
