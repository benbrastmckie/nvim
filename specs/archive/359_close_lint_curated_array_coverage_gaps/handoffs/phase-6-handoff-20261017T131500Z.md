## What NOT to Try

- Do not add a new fixture at a real curated-array path without checking ALL FOUR curated
  arrays (`IN_SCOPE_RELATIVE_PATHS`, `OWNERSHIP_IN_SCOPE_RELATIVE_PATHS`,
  `BOUNDED_WAIT_IN_SCOPE_RELATIVE_PATHS`, `MODIFIED_FILES_IN_SCOPE_RELATIVE_PATHS`) for that
  exact path first -- membership in more than one is common and each produces its own FAIL if
  the fixture is left minimal.
