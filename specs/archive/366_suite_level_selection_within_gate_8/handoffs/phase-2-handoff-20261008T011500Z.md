## Key Decisions Made

- `suite_selection_classify`'s textual heuristic excludes pure-comment lines (`^\s*#`) from both
  the touched-basename scan and the `.claude`-reference scan, to avoid a suite's own header prose
  being mistaken for its subject-resolution code.
- Public API settled as four functions: `suite_selection_select`, `suite_selection_unmapped`,
  `suite_selection_classify`, `suite_selection_select_annotated` (the last a convenience wrapper
  combining the first and third).
