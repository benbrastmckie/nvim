## What NOT to Try

- Do NOT reintroduce a `set -e`/`set +e` toggle anywhere in this script. `run_sandboxed()`
  originally had one around the `timeout` invocation; since `set -e`/`+e` are GLOBAL shell
  attributes (not function-scoped), turning `-e` ON there silently activated errexit for the
  REST of the script, aborting classify_verdict()'s substring-matching pipelines on their
  expected non-matches (a `grep -qF` that legitimately returns 1 when a string isn't present).
  This produced a silent, no-verdict, exit-1 failure that was hard to diagnose -- `bash -x`
  tracing was required to find it. The fix was to remove the toggle entirely; the script only
  ever runs under `set -uo pipefail`.
- Do not try to literally separate Comparator's stdout from its stderr in captured logs -- the
  `--pty` wrapper makes this structurally impossible; classify_verdict() correctly relies on
  substring matching against the single merged stream instead.
