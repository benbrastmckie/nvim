## Key Decisions Made

- Skip-reason `Note:`/fail-open `WARNING:` lines are unconditional `echo ... >&2`, NOT routed
  through the `say()` helper — they must survive `--quiet` (Gate 8 calls run-all.sh with
  `--quiet`, and Gate 8's own stall was the motivating incident, so observability there matters
  most).
- `RUN_ALL_WAS_NESTED_AT_ENTRY` is captured BEFORE the pre-existing unconditional
  `export RUN_ALL_NESTED=1`, since after that line every read of the variable reports "1"
  regardless of whether this invocation was originally nested.
- Deployed `task-lock.sh` resolution mirrors `MODE` exactly (deployed -> `$DEPLOY_SCRIPTS_ROOT/
  task-lock.sh`; source-store -> two levels above `$EXTENSIONS_ROOT` then `.claude/scripts/
  task-lock.sh`), placed immediately after the existing mode-detection if/else block.

## What NOT to Try

- Do not route the gate-lock `Note:`/`WARNING:` lines through `say()` — this would silently
  suppress them under `--quiet`, defeating the observability requirement for exactly the Gate 8
  call site that motivated this task.
