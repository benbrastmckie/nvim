## IMPORTANT — Labeling correction on the contended measurement (record this verbatim in the
## implementation summary; do not let it disappear as an incidental fix)

The `GATE_MUTEX_ACQUIRE_BUDGET_MS=0` measurement window was initially mislabeled as a
"lock-disabled baseline." It is NOT that. In that window, the mkdir race's WINNER still
legitimately acquired and held the mutex exactly as the generous-budget path would — only the
LOSER's zero-length wait budget was defeated, causing it to fail open (with a named `WARNING:`
line and an `ERROR: timed out waiting ...` line naming the winning holder). This is fail-open
evidence for an ACTIVE bracket, not a genuinely-disabled-coordination baseline. The actual
non-vacuousness-of-serialization claim (two invocations overlap when coordination is truly
absent) is already established by Phase 4's own fixture case, which disables coordination by
removing the deployed `task-lock.sh` entirely — a different mechanism from zeroing a budget.
Both are valid and valuable; they are not interchangeable, and the plan body's "Contended
Measurement" subsection now states this distinction explicitly. Scratchpad artifacts were
renamed from `measure-lockdisabled-*` to `measure-failopen-budget0-*` to match.

## What NOT to Try

- Do not cite the `GATE_MUTEX_ACQUIRE_BUDGET_MS=0` measurement window as non-vacuousness
  evidence for the serialization claim — it demonstrates a different property (fail-open on
  budget exhaustion for an active bracket), and Phase 4's fixture is the actual non-vacuousness
  evidence.
