## Key Decisions Made

- `enumerate_untracked_matches` stayed the single source of untracked-set membership; it is now
  called for both the `*.sh` and `*.md` patterns (previously only `*.sh`), split in bash into
  `UNTRACKED_CANDIDATES` (non-test `.sh`, feeds JSON/warning) and `UNTRACKED_TEST_SUITES` (test
  `.sh`, feeds only the `--size-line` total) from one shared `*.sh` shell-out, plus a separate
  `UNTRACKED_MD` for the markdown pattern.
- `--size-line`'s `untracked=N` is one combined total across all three patterns, not a
  per-pattern breakdown, keeping the one-line contract simple.
- JSON mode's `untracked_count`/`untracked_paths` are scoped to the non-test `.sh` population
  that is actually in `scripts`/`ranking` (i.e. `UNTRACKED_CANDIDATES`), not the broader
  `--size-line` total — the two are deliberately different scopes for different consumers.
- `usage()`'s hardcoded `sed -n '2,NNNp'` range was shifted from `163` to `176` to preserve its
  prior (already truncated, pre-existing, out-of-scope) cutoff point after the header grew by
  ~13 lines; the truncation itself was not fixed, since fixing it was not in this phase's task
  list and no test covers `--help` output content.
