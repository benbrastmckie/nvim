## Key Decisions Made

- The progress label itself is used as the bracketed stderr tag (e.g. `[gate-lock] waiting
  30s ...`) rather than hardcoding "gate-lock" in the generic primitive — keeps
  `acquire_named_mutex` genuinely generic while matching the plan's example output exactly.
- `GATE_MUTEX_PROGRESS_INTERVAL_S` is read directly inside `acquire_named_mutex` (not threaded
  as a 6th positional) since no other family populates a progress label today; documented as
  inherited-by-any-future-caller rather than gate-specific.
- New `.gate-lock` family and `cmd_gate_acquire`/`cmd_gate_release` placed immediately after the
  `.commit-lock` family/`cmd_commit_release`, before the `claim-acquire` block — same ordering
  precedent as `.scope-lock` -> `.commit-lock`.
