  per line) rather than a full `json.dump` re-serialization, to keep diffs minimal and avoid
  reformatting the entire file (an earlier attempt via full JSON round-trip caused ~65 lines of
  unrelated reformatting; reverted and redone with surgical string replacement).

  `PostToolUse` array, mirroring root-files/settings.json's own ordering.

## What NOT to Try
- Do not use `python3 json.load`/`json.dump` round-trip on these settings files for small edits —
  it reformats the entire file and produces a huge diff. Use targeted string substitution instead.
