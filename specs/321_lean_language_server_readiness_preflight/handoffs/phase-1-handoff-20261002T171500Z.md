  itself fails, or EVERY matched candidate's environ was unreadable.

## What NOT to Try

- Do not fake a background "server" process by `cp`-ing a coreutils multicall binary (e.g.
  `sleep`) to a renamed path and backgrounding it — on this NixOS machine coreutils dispatches
  on `argv[0]`/basename, so the renamed copy fails at exec time with "unknown program" and the
  backgrounded process exits immediately, never appearing in `ps`. Use a small bash script with
  a `sleep` body instead (confirmed working, used in the manual verification above and should be
  reused for Phase 2's fixture J/K/L fake-server helper).
