## What NOT to Try

- Do not pass `index-entries.json` (or any file) to `git-commit-phase.sh`/`git-commit-scoped.sh`
  while it carries a second, unrelated dirty hunk you want to leave uncommitted — `git commit --
  <pathspec>` RE-READS the working tree at commit time regardless of prior `git add`/`git apply
  --cached` staging, so a partially-staged index does not protect you. Isolate by temporarily
  reverting the foreign hunk's own lines in the working file, commit, then restore them.
