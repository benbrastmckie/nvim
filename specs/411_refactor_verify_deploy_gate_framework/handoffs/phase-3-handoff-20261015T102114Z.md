**Current State**: Phase 3 is `[COMPLETED]`. `scripts/measure-gate-costs.sh` exists with a

classes (free/moderate/slow, boundaries in its own header). The authoritative, fully-clean,
idle 23-gate table is at `progress/phase-3-gate-cost-model.csv`.

**Key Decisions Made**:
- DEVIATION (3.1): used `date +%s%3N` wall-clock delta instead of `/usr/bin/time` (the plan's
  literal suggestion), because `/usr/bin/time` does not exist as that literal path on this
  NixOS measurement host -- confirmed live. This reuses `tests/run-all.sh`'s own already-proven
  timing technique rather than depending on a non-portable external binary.
- The load guard was LIVE-VALIDATED by a real, severe contention episode during this dispatch
  (1-minute load average spiked to 50+ from concurrent sibling /orchestrate sessions). The
  first full 23-gate sweep's gate8 measurement showed a 3x run-to-run spread (624s vs 1794s) and
  was correctly flagged contaminated by the post-measurement check; it was DISCARDED, not
  merged or averaged in. A second, fully clean sweep (1min load ~1.0-1.6 throughout, both
  pre- and post-check passing) produced the authoritative table.
- `suite-cost-hints.txt` regeneration needed 3 attempts due to recurring MILD tail-end load
  creep (2.4-2.6 right at the ~10-minute sequential 2x147-suite sweep's completion, not a severe
  spike). Accepted the 3rd attempt per the file's own documented advisory-only nature, but
  recorded an explicit caveat (progress file, objective 3 note): that file's
  `test-verify-deploy-context-budget.sh,228595` row specifically should be treated with
  caution (disproportionately contention-sensitive due to its nested verify-deploy.sh
  invocations) -- gate-cost-model.csv is the authoritative artifact, not this file.

**What NOT to Try**:
- Do not trust a gate-cost or suite-cost figure that was not captured idle -- re-run
  `measure-gate-costs.sh` (optionally scoped to just the suspect gate via `--gate N`) once
  `/proc/loadavg`'s 1-minute figure is comfortably under 2.0, rather than patching in a
  contaminated number by hand.
- Do not treat `suite-cost-hints.txt`'s `test-verify-deploy-context-budget.sh` row as
  authoritative without re-measurement -- it is the one figure in that file most likely still
  carrying tail-contamination skew.
