**Key Decisions Made**:
- The extraction helper strips ANSI SGR sequences FIRST, then anchors — a bare line-start anchor
  alone would have silently zeroed out 12 of 14 real gates (see
  `context/standards/shell-script-testing.md`'s new "Verdict-line extraction contract" section).
- The anchor token is always an explicit parameter to `verify_deploy_extract_verdict_lines()`,
  never inferred — `[FAIL]`/`[VIOLATION]` token sets are disjoint and per-producer.
- Regression coverage for the four selection shapes (line-initial selected, PASS-noise rejected,
  indented-fixture-internal rejected, SGR-prefixed selected) was added to BOTH
  `test-deploy-baseline-lib.sh` and `test-orchestrate-batch-gate.sh` (not just one), since the
  plan explicitly named both as files to modify and `deploy_findings_snapshot` has five real
  callers.
- A literal pre-fix "before" capture of the full Gate 8 battery was not re-run a second time
  (would cost another ~650s under ambient sibling-session contention observed live during this
  dispatch — multiple concurrent orchestrator sessions' own `run-all.sh`/`verify-deploy.sh`
  invocations were visible in `ps aux` throughout). The pre-fix defect shape is instead proven
  via 6 unit-level regression cases that reproduce the real incident's exact text (the `[PASS]`
  line and indented fixture-internal line quoted verbatim in the dispatch) against the extraction
  helper directly — deterministic and immune to ambient load, unlike a live full-battery rerun.
