## What NOT to Try

- Do not diff suite output with a naive per-line `[FAIL]`-substring count. One suite
  (`test-verify-deploy-context-budget.sh`) has an already-broken (pre-existing, in
  `known-failures.txt`) diagnostic case that dumps a live `gate3`/`doc-lint` scan of the real
  repository as part of explaining why its own fixture baseline isn't clean; that scan's sub-text
  varies with deploy-tree freshness and with concurrent-sibling-task untracked files sitting in
  the shared working tree (this is a multi-task `/orchestrate` batch — other tasks' untracked
  artifacts were visible throughout this phase). This produced an apparent mismatch under a naive
  line-counting diff even though the suite's own authoritative `Passed`/`Failed` tally never
  changed. Use the suite's own tally line as ground truth, not a raw line/tag count.
- Do not expect `run-all.sh`'s background execution to stay in the foreground for its full
  runtime in this environment — this sandbox auto-backgrounds any Bash call running past roughly
  180s regardless of the declared `timeout` parameter (observed three times), diverging from the
  documented 120s/600s contract. The working pattern: launch detached with a captured `pid`,
  then poll with short (`sleep 100-170`), quick, single-shot `kill -0 "$pid"` checks that each
  return well under the ~180s auto-background threshold, rather than one long blocking wait loop.
