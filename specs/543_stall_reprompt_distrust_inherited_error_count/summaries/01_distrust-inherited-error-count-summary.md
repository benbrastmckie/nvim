## Overview

The stall/strand re-prompt remedy ordered foreground re-verification but said nothing about where
the resumer's failure inventory comes from, so a resumer handed a partial error count from the
interrupting context could reconcile against that number and stop early. This implementation adds
one obligation — the re-run's own output is the sole inventory, and any inherited count is
unverified and non-exhaustive — to the canonical "Loop obligation" list and to three of the four
remedy-emission sites in the postflight script, then pins the new clause with a non-vacuous
content assertion in the test suite and propagates everything to the deployed tree by deploy only.

## Decisions

- The addition was phrased as a new numbered item in the "Loop obligation" list (rather than a
  clause appended to item 1) so it reads as its own discrete instruction to the resumer, parallel
  to the existing attribution/closure/artifact-write items.
- The `stranded_suspected` notice's reasoned exclusion (item 4 above) is accepted on its own
  stated grounds: duplicating prose across two notices that must stay in sync is the exact defect
  class this change exists to avoid reproducing.
